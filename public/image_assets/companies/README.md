# Company Logos (Letters of Interest)

Place the following logo files (preferably SVG format) in this directory:

## Required Logo Files:

- `solinas.svg` - Solinas company logo
- `xyma.svg` - XYMA Analytics company logo
- `neomotion.svg` - NeoMotion company logo
- `kcat.svg` - KCat company logo

## Notes:
- Use SVG format for best quality and scalability
- PNG format also works (transparent background recommended)
- Logos will be displayed at 96x96px (w-24 h-24)
- These logos represent companies that have expressed interest in partnering with BlazeUp
- Make sure logos are high quality and appropriately sized
